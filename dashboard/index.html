The road to the peak

What each day tells you about the week's peak

DayAverage views% of peakGain to peakGain rangePrediction errorError in views

Prediction error tests each week in turn: it multiplies that day by the average gain from the other weeks, then compares the result with the real peak. A lower error means that day is a better early signal.

Average the past

DayActualSimple averageRecent-weightedPace-adjusted

Simple average gives every past week equal weight. Recent-weighted makes each newer week count more, by the tilt you set. Pace-adjusted scales the recent-weighted forecast to match the days already reported this week.
